# Ex.No.9 Exploration of Prompting Techniques for Video Generation

# Date:2.9.2026
# Reg. No.:212223030011


## AIM

To explore different prompting techniques for AI-based video generation by creating videos from textual descriptions and progressively refining prompts to control the subject, environment, motion, camera movement, lighting, composition, and visual style.

---

## OBJECTIVE

To understand how prompt refinement can improve the quality and controllability of AI-generated videos by incorporating detailed descriptions of visual elements, actions, temporal changes, camera movements, lighting, and cinematic style.

---

## TOOLS / AI MODELS USED

* AI-based text-to-video generation tool
* ChatGPT for prompt design and refinement
* Tools / AI Models Used: AI-based text-to-video generation tool such as OpenAI Sora, Google Veo, Runway, Kling

---

## PROCEDURE

1. Select a video scenario to reproduce or generate using an AI video-generation tool.
2. Observe the reference video and identify its important visual and motion characteristics.
3. Analyze the video based on:

   * Subject and objects
   * Environment and background
   * Colors and textures
   * Lighting and shadows
   * Subject movement
   * Object movement
   * Camera movement
   * Perspective and composition
   * Duration and sequence of actions
   * Artistic or cinematic style
4. Create a basic prompt describing the main subject and action.
5. Refine the prompt by adding environmental details, colors, lighting, movement, and atmosphere.
6. Create an advanced prompt specifying camera movement, subject motion, timing, perspective, transitions, and visual style.
7. Generate the video using the selected AI video-generation model.
8. Compare the generated video with the intended scene or reference video.
9. Identify differences in motion, composition, lighting, timing, and visual consistency.
10. Refine the prompt and generate an improved version.
11. Save the final video and document the prompts and observations.

# PROMPT PROGRESSION ANALYSIS

| Prompt Level  | Main Characteristics                                                | Expected Output                          |
| ------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------- | ---------------------------------------- |
| **Basic**     | Subject and main action only                                        | General scene with limited control       |
| **Refined**   | Adds environment, colors, objects, and atmosphere                   | Better scene composition                 |
| **Advanced**  | Adds motion, camera movement, lighting, textures, timing, and style | Greater control over video generation    |
| **Iterative** | Corrects motion and visual inconsistencies based on previous output | Improved temporal and visual consistency |

---

# VIDEO GENERATION PARAMETERS TO CONSIDER

When designing prompts for video generation, the following parameters should be considered:

* **Subject:** Main person, object, vehicle, animal, or machine
* **Action:** What the subject is doing
* **Motion:** Direction, speed, and type of movement
* **Camera:** Pan, tilt, zoom, tracking, dolly, or static shot
* **Composition:** Position of the subject and surrounding elements
* **Lighting:** Natural, cinematic, dramatic, studio, or artificial lighting
* **Environment:** Indoor, outdoor, industrial, urban, underwater, etc.
* **Texture:** Metal, glass, fabric, water, concrete, vegetation, etc.
* **Atmosphere:** Fog, mist, rain, dust, underwater particles, etc.
* **Style:** Photorealistic, cinematic, animation, documentary, or artistic
* **Temporal Consistency:** Consistent object appearance and smooth movement throughout the video

---

# OBSERVATION

The basic prompts produced the intended subject and general action but provided limited control over the environment and movement. Refining the prompts with additional visual information improved the scene composition and appearance.

The advanced prompts provided greater control by specifying subject movement, camera motion, lighting, textures, environmental effects, and cinematic style. This resulted in more coherent and visually detailed video sequences.

# RESULT

Videos were generated using progressively refined prompts. The experiment demonstrated that adding detailed descriptions of subjects, actions, environments, camera movements, lighting, textures, and cinematic characteristics provides greater control over AI-generated video content.

---

# CONCLUSION

The experiment demonstrated that effective prompt engineering is essential for controlling AI-based video generation. Basic prompts are useful for establishing the main subject and action, while refined prompts provide additional control over the environment, appearance, and atmosphere. Advanced prompts that specify motion, camera movement, lighting, composition, textures, and temporal consistency can produce more coherent and realistic video sequences. Iterative refinement further improves the quality and consistency of the generated output.
